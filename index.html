I am a PhD student in computer science at Örebro University and a WASP fellow, working with Johannes Andreas Stork and Pedro Zuidberg Dos Martires.

My research focuses on sequential decision-making, particularly transfer learning and the use of foundation models. I am interested in extracting structured knowledge from existing data to enable more efficient learning and adaptation to new tasks.

Before my PhD, I studied vision-based navigation for UAVs with Hyondong Oh at UNIST, where I obtained my bachelor's and master's degrees in mechanical engineering. Earlier, I represented Azerbaijan at international Physics Olympiads and received several national and international awards.
